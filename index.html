04 — Personal Log

Field Journal

The biggest shift for me this internship wasn't a framework — it was learning to trust evidence over assumption. My first Lighthouse audit on Katas Raj reported a Performance score of 37. I could have submitted that as fact. Instead, running it again in a clean incognito window showed the real number was 52 — Chrome extensions had quietly corrupted the first read.

Later, compressing my hero image only moved the score to 58 — disappointing, until I actually read Lighthouse's own diagnostic breakdown instead of assuming compression was the whole fix. The image was still ten times larger in pixel dimensions than its display size. Resizing it properly took Performance to 80. The tool told me exactly what was wrong, twice. I just had to slow down and read it.

The second lesson came from review, not instruments. Reading a peer's site showed me gaps in my own work I couldn't see by looking at it alone — no live interactivity, no visible sourcing, no location context. That produced the strongest single change I made all internship: a depth-intensity slider that recalculates my 3D mesh's geometry live, built because I saw someone else do something more interactive and asked why mine couldn't do the same.

— Amna
